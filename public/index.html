The problem

Right now, every question assumes someone is available.

WiFi, arrival times, gate codes: most questions come up villa after villa, guest after guest — but there always needs to be someone to answer them, at any hour, in the language of whoever's asking.

An emergency, at any hour

A leak or a breakdown doesn't call ahead: without round-the-clock responsiveness, it's a rough night for the guest, and one less review for the villa.

How it works

The concierge answers first. You, only when needed.

Each villa has its own knowledge base — WiFi, arrival, amenities, recommendations. The virtual concierge uses it to answer your guests, in their own language, at any hour.

Step 1

The guest reaches out

They scan the QR code posted in the villa and land directly on the chat — in their own language, at any hour, with no account or app to install.

Step 2

The concierge answers

It knows the WiFi, the schedule, the amenities and the local recommendations for the villa — and replies right away, without ever making anything up.

Step 3

If it doesn't know, you're notified right away

No waiting: the alert goes out immediately over WhatsApp and email, with the guest's exact question.

And for anything specific to a villa — a pet allowed under certain conditions, a particular door code, a homeowners' association rule — the "Custom questions" category lets you add as many tailored questions and answers as you need, on top of the 24 guided categories.

Each item can also include a photo — the door code pad, where the wifi box is, which button on the remote.

Every villa also gets its own QR code, generated automatically and ready to print — to stick in the entryway, the kitchen or the welcome book. The guest scans it, types their question: no app to download, no account to create.
